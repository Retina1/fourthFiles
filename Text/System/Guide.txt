##GuideAilments
Ailments[X]

##GuideAilmentDesc
All ailments share a formula[N]
for affliction odds.[N]
B = Base Chance[N]
U = User's Luc + 10[N]
T = Target's Luc + 10[N]
Odds = B * U / T[N]
[N]
A unit can have a single[N]
ailment at a time, and can[N]
also have one or more binds.[N]
Ailments have a hierarchy -[N]
a unit can only have their[N]
current ailment reapplied or[N]
a higher ailment afflicted,[N]
not a lower ailment afflicted.[N]
The hierarchy is:[N]
Petrify > Curse > Poison >[N]
Sleep > Berserk >[N]
Paralysis > Blind[N]
Ex. A unit that's Berserked[N]
can't be Paralyzed or Blinded,[N]
but can be Slept, Poisoned,[N]
Cursed, Petrified, or have[N]
its Berserk duration reset.[N]
[N]
Head Bind: Head bound units[N]
have halved Magic and lose[N]
the ability to use Arts.[N]
[N]
Arm Bind: Arm bound units[N]
have halved Strength and[N]
are unable to crit.[N]
[N]
Leg Bind: Leg bound units[N]
can't dodge and have their[N]
movement zeroed out.[N]
[N]
Blind: Blinded units can't[N]
dodge, and their hit rate[N]
is divided by 3 (after the[N]
hitrate floor).[N]
[N]
Paralysis: Paralyzed units[N]
can't dodge, and on even[N]
numbered turns, can't act.[N]
[N]
Berserk: Berserked units will[N]
attack anything in range, be[N]
it a friend or a foe.[N]
[N]
Sleep: Asleep units can't dodge[N]
or act, take 1.5x damage from[N]
attacks, and wake up after[N]
being attacked.[N]
[N]
Poison: Poisoned units take[N]
(MaxHP/5) + 5 damage a turn.[N]
This damage can kill.[N]
[N]
Curse: Cursed units take all[N]
combat damage they deal.[N]
This can't kill the cursed.[N]
[N]
Petrify: Petrified units can't[N]
dodge or act, and they face[N]
1.5x crit from attacks.[X]
